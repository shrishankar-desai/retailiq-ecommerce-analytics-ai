PROJECT CASE STUDY

About the platform

Real historical data. Practical analytics. Transparent modeling.

Business objective

Help e-commerce teams identify products attracting attention, describe shopper engagement, and explore relevant item recommendations based on implicit feedback.

What we built

Automated processing of raw shopping events and time-varying category snapshots, a browser-based dashboard, item-to-item collaborative filtering, popularity fallback, and time-based offline evaluation.

Deliverables

Downloadable product and activity datasets, reproducible training code, API endpoints, unit tests, and documented model limitations.

Important limitations

Observations date from 2015. Product names and monetary sales figures are unavailable. Product attributes are anonymized, and recommendations use historical co-interaction instead of modern user embeddings.

Visitors with no prior history receive popular-item suggestions. Offline ranking performance does not establish commercial lift.

Dataset and licensing

Retailrocket e-commerce dataset, published for recommender research and licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Verify permitted usage before any commercial redistribution or client deployment.
